RCM Go-Live Portal

Help & Reference Guide

Overview

The RCM Go-Live Portal helps you plan and track the complete setup of a US Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) operation. It guides you through ten phases—from defining service scope to pilot go-live—with checklists, cost planning, and issue tracking.

Getting Started

Login

Sign in with your credentials on the login page.

Dashboard

After login, you land on the Dashboard. Here you can:

  • Track progress – The circular progress ring shows how many sections are complete for the selected client.
  • Switch clients – Use the Client dropdown in the header to work with different clients.
  • Add a client – Click the + button next to the Client dropdown to create a new client.

Dashboard Sections

  • Client summary cards – Total clients, Planned, In progress, Live.
  • Clients by status – Bar showing Planned vs In progress vs Live.
  • Open issues – Issues logged in sections, with links to open the relevant section.
  • Go-Live Plan sections – Cards for each phase. Use View for a quick preview (popup) or Open & Edit to work through the checklist.

Working on Sections

Each section contains checklists and forms. You can:

  • Help – For phases with detailed documentation, opens a read-only guide (Phase 1 and Phase 2).
  • View – Opens a popup with the section description and checklist summary.
  • Open & Edit – Opens the full section page with all items, forms, detail tables, and issues.
  • Save Progress – Manually save your work (auto-save also runs when you make changes).
  • Mark as Complete & Continue – Marks the section done and updates the progress ring.

Progress is stored in your browser per client. Use the back link to return to the Dashboard.

Go-Live Plan Phases

  • Phase 2 Office SetupCost estimation and facility planning
  • Phase 3 Discovery & Information GatheringCapture client details, systems, and compliance
  • Phase 4 Systems & TechnologyPMS, EMR, clearinghouse, VPN, and tools
  • Phase 5 SOPs & ComplianceDocument processes and BAAs  View SOPs →
  • Phase 6 Reports & KPIsDefine daily, denial, AR, and other reports
  • Phase 7 Staffing & TrainingHire, onboard, and qualify the team
  • Phase 8 Pilot & Soft Go-LivePilot run and sign-off
  • Phase 9 Risks & MitigationIdentify and address key risks
  • Phase 10 Office Setup & Capacity PlanningLocation comparison, Infopark costing, infrastructure

Issues & Alerts

You can add issues within sections (e.g. blockers, action items). Issues appear in:

  • The Open issues panel on the Dashboard
  • The Issues block at the bottom of each section page

Each issue can have severity, assignee, and resolution status.

Multi-Client Support

The portal supports multiple clients. Each client has its own:

  • Section progress and checklists
  • Completed sections
  • Detail tables and office setup data
  • Issues

Use the Client dropdown in the Dashboard header to switch between clients.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser. It is not sent to a server. Clearing browser data will reset progress. For team use, consider exporting or backing up data if needed.

Need More Help?

Contact your administrator or the RCM setup team for questions about content, phases, or workflows.
